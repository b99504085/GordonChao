# Trial Balloon Cartoon Opening Production Plan

## Current MVP Limitation

The current After Effects script is a timeline generator. It uses flat PNG character art, simple position animation, fade cuts, cards, and text. This is useful for checking story flow, timing, and copy, but it will not look like a polished 3D cartoon animation by itself.

## Target Look

- Cute 2.5D / 3D cartoon feeling
- Characters have volume, soft shading, and natural movement
- Yellow acts energetic and persuasive
- Blue acts calm, slightly special, and quietly confident
- Pink acts curious, sneaky, and observant
- Final shot freezes into a polished title frame

## Recommended Production Route

### Step 1: Character Rig Preparation

Create layered character assets for each mascot:

- Body
- Left arm
- Right arm
- Feet
- Face
- Eyes
- Mouth
- Accessories, such as balloon, star, magnifying glass

Transparent PNG or layered PSD files are ideal.

### Step 2: After Effects Rigging

Use After Effects with Puppet Pins or a character rigging plugin such as Duik Angela.

Required motions:

- Yellow: bounce forward, wave, point, balloon float
- Blue: blink, small head tilt, star sparkle, gentle step
- Pink: sneak in, lift magnifying glass, eye focus, small bounce

### Step 3: 2.5D Depth

Add subtle depth without full 3D modeling:

- Separate foreground, midground, and background layers
- Enable 3D layers in After Effects
- Add a camera with slight push-in movement
- Add shadows under characters
- Add small parallax movement to bubbles, arrows, stars, and cards

### Step 4: Final Animation Timing

Keep the 20-second structure:

1. Normal chat
2. Question card
3. Crowd-Puller action
4. Outlier action
5. Shadow observation
6. Role reveal and title freeze

### Step 5: Polish

Add:

- Motion blur
- Ease-in and ease-out keyframes
- Squash and stretch on jumps
- Sound effects for card flips, pop-ups, sparkles, magnifying glass sweep
- Background music with playful tension

## AI-Assisted Alternative

If fully rigging the characters takes too long, generate each scene as a short image-to-video clip using the character references, then assemble clips in After Effects or CapCut.

Recommended clips:

1. Three mascots chatting, cute cartoon style
2. Question card popping up
3. Yellow mascot encouraging everyone toward one answer
4. Blue mascot calmly choosing a different answer
5. Pink mascot observing a player through a magnifying glass
6. Three mascots posing with Trial Balloon title

After generation, use After Effects for:

- Text overlays
- Timing
- Role cards
- Final title lockup
- Music and sound effects

## Best Next Step

Use the current MVP only as a storyboard timing reference. For the real opening animation, the next practical step is to create layered character rigs or AI-generated motion clips based on the three mascot designs.
